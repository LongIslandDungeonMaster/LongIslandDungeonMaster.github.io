Where the Road Ends

Four wildernesses. Four encounter tables. Four fights worth having — an Adventure Keeper you roll in your browser, yours to keep.

Download the Keeper or open it in your browser first

One offline file. No account, no app — it runs from your laptop at the table, forever.

Roll when the road runs out. Each wilderness — forest, swamp, mountain, coast — has a d10 of encounters where every row is a creature, a situation, and a fork, not just a fight. And every table is capped by a fully framed encounter you can run cold: the druid who treed a foreman for the right reason, the drowned ferryman who charges one true thing per passenger, two grieving harpies over a killing stair, a three-sided wreck against the tide. Dice, trackers, and both clocks are built in. It’s the companion to the free Wilderness Pack — that one fills the quiet miles; this is what’s waiting when the quiet ends.
