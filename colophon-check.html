The Salt Pan Vault

A crossing that is paid for in memory.

Run It

A three day crossing of a dead sea, a shrine in the middle of it, and a price paid in memory rather than coin.

Below the waterline, three Brine-Drowned stand upright on the bottom, facing up. They do not move while anyone is looking at the water.
