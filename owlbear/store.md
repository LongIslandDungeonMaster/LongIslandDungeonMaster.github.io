# Gnasher's Satchel

A 5e character sheet that opens inside your Owlbear room. Click the satchel in the
top left and your character is there beside the map, instead of in a second tab you
keep losing.

There is no account to make and no server holding your character. The sheet saves in
your own browser. If the wifi drops in the middle of a session it keeps working, and
so does the rest of the sheet.

## What it does

- The sheet itself: abilities, skills, saves, proficiency, armour class, hit points, hit dice, conditions
- A dice roller wired to the sheet, so a skill check rolls with the right modifier already in it
- Spells, slots and prepared lists
- A rules lookup you can read at the table without leaving the room
- Character creation from nothing, or type in a character you already play

## What it does not do

Worth knowing before you install it, because these are real limits and not a roadmap.

- **It does not talk to the map.** Moving a token will not change the sheet, and the sheet will not move a token.
- **It does not share your sheet with the table.** Every player runs their own copy.
- **It is 5e.** If you play Shadowdark, Dragonbane or Land of Eem, the sheets already in this store will serve you better than this one will.

## Where it came from

The Satchel is the player half of a pair. The other half is Gnasher's Grimoire, a
game master's tool built the same way, out of one file with nothing to sign into.
Both are free and they stay free.

You can read about both, and download either one to keep, at
[longislanddungeonmaster.com](https://longislanddungeonmaster.com/satchel).
