The Mercy Hives

Her honey cures anything. It is pressed from the town’s dead, and every jar costs you a memory of someone you loved. A free encounter you can run cold.

Download the Encounter or open the PDF in your browser first

One print-ready PDF. No account, no app. Yours to keep, run it tonight.

A widow keeps thirty hives outside Threshing Cross, and her honey heals anything: wounds, fevers, what medicine cannot. It heals because the hive runs on remembrance, and every jar takes a memory of someone you loved. The fever broke. The graveyard went quiet. And the town slowly stopped being able to picture the people under the stones. This is a villain you talk to, not through: run her as a confession, not a fight. Built for four characters of levels 3 to 5, with honest encounter math, three real endings, scaling for levels 1 to 5, and a d8 of what the comb remembers. Five print-ready pages, run cold in half an hour.
