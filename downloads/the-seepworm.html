The Seepworm

A CR 1 parasite that hunts the water your party is carrying. It bites once for one point of damage, and that is the entire design.

Open the Creature Keeper

One file, running in your browser. The worm is already in the initiative tracker, all three clocks are built, and all three tables roll themselves. Offline, no account, yours to keep.

Or drop it into Gnasher’s Grimoire

Save the Keeper, then drag the file onto the Grimoire shelf. The worm, the three tables and the witness land beside everything else you run from.

Most low-level monsters threaten hit points, and a party with a cleric stops caring about hit points somewhere in the second session. This one threatens the water. A seepworm smells damp through sixty feet of solid rock, surfaces under whatever is wettest in the camp, takes one bite for one point of piercing damage, and burrows back out before anyone has finished standing up. The wound is trivial. Then the party guards the water, and the worm takes the next wettest thing in the camp, which is a person: a DC 13 Constitution save, and on a failure it is under the skin. A host gains nothing from drinking, because the worm drinks first, and picks up a level of Exhaustion every long rest that will not come off. And a dead seepworm holds every drop it ever took, roughly a day’s water, drinkable, seeded with young. In a dry camp, someone always drinks. You get the full stat block, three tables, a witness worth talking to, and a Keeper that runs the whole thing from a browser tab. Free to use at your table under CC BY 4.0.
