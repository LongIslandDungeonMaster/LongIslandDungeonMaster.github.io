Cast a spell and pick the slot; concentrate on one and the sheet asks for the save when you take a hit. Every rule, action, feat, feature and item in the free SRD 5.2.1 is a search away, with the dice in the text ready to roll.

Install to rolling, in ten steps

The same walkthrough lives inside the app, under How to use on your character shelf.

  1. Install it. Open the Satchel. iPhone: Safari's Share button, then Add to Home Screen. Android: Chrome's menu, then Add to Home screen, then Install.
  2. Make a character with + New Character: name, class, level, and the Standard array button for instant ability scores. Three minutes, playable.
  3. Or bring your D&D Beyond character. Set it to Public on dndbeyond.com (Manage, then Character Settings, then Privacy), then paste your character's link into the Satchel's Import. It opens your data page in a new tab; select all, copy, tap Paste, done. You carry your own data; the Satchel never fetches it for you.
  4. Or bring your Foundry character: right-click the actor, Export Data, and feed the .json to Import.
  5. Roll by tapping. Any ability, save, skill, or attack rolls itself, bonus included. Set Adv or Dis on the dice tray first when it matters.
  6. Run the fight from the Fight tab. Damage, Heal, and Temp HP live inside the hit-point plaque; at 0 HP it becomes death saves, and a Healed button waits there for your cleric. Conditions, slots, hit dice, class resources, and rests are all one tap.
  7. Level up in the Study. Tap Level up: the Satchel shows what the level brings, asks for your subclass or your Ability Score Improvement or feat, files the features you gain, and moves every number that should move.
  8. Join the table. Tap the wax seal, type the short code your DM reads out, tap Join. The seal glows when you're linked and remembers the code for next week.
  9. Play. A YOUR TURN banner takes the top of the screen when the fight reaches you. DM handouts and whispers land in your Journal, where you can rename, tag, and annotate them.
  10. Keep notes. Journal tab: dated Session Notes, free Campaign Notes pages, and your handout shelf.
  11. Back it up. Journal tab, Export .satchel. One small file is your whole character; Import brings it back on any device.

For DMs: light the table in five steps

You host from Gnasher's Grimoire; your players just point their Satchels at it.

  1. Open Gnasher's Grimoire and tap Theater in the menu bar.
  2. Tap Open the table by code and read the code aloud. Each player types it into their Satchel's wax seal. (No venue internet? Same room mode or your phone's hotspot.)
  3. Run combat exactly as you always do; the tracker drives every paired Satchel by itself, turn banners included.
  4. Push handouts (text or images) from the Theater panel; pick one name in the To row to whisper it privately.
  5. Phones that doze show as resting seats. Keep playing; they catch up the moment they wake.
